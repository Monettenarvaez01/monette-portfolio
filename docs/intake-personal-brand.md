# Intake - Building My Personal Brand

**Collection:** Independent Projects · **Label:** Independent · **Status:** D - Draft (waiting for Monette's material)

This case study is about your own LinkedIn presence: the decisions you made, what you tried, what you noticed and what the numbers show. It is your work and your data, so it needs no client permission. It is **not** client work and will never be described as a client campaign or as proof of client results.

Fill in what you have. Rough notes are fine. Leave anything blank that you don't have; blanks are never filled in for you. I'll turn approved notes into page copy and show it to you before it goes anywhere.

---

## Rules for this case study

- **Real only.** Every post, decision, observation and number comes from your actual LinkedIn activity.
- **Numbers exactly as LinkedIn shows them,** with the date range and the page they come from. No rounding up, projections, growth percentages you calculated yourself (unless you say so and show the inputs), or comparisons with other people.
- **Small numbers are fine.** An experiment with no clear result is still worth showing for the thinking behind it.
- **Correlation, not cause.** Pages will say "after I changed X, Y happened in this period", not "X caused Y", unless you have a reason to believe it.
- **No client crossover.** Nothing about any client, client content or client results appears here, including LinkedIn content you drafted for someone else.
- **Other people's privacy.** Names, photos, comments, messages and profile details of other people are removed or blurred in anything you share.
- **Screenshots stay private** unless you choose one to show. Keep them in `private/` on your computer (ignored by git) and send me only the figures.
